# Uncertainty and Evidence Review

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Megan Krone,
*Navigating the Dissertation Writing Process*, and Sara Rubinelli,
*Institutional Health Communication in the Information Age*.

## Review each material requirement

Check the claim, source or stakeholder evidence, interpretation, uncertainty,
affected user, acceptance test, and owner. Mark the statement as observed,
inferred, proposed, or unresolved. Do not hide contradictory evidence inside a
confident requirement.

## Communication of uncertainty

When evidence is incomplete or changing, state what is known, what is uncertain,
what would change the decision, when it will be reviewed, and what safe action is
available meanwhile. Use plain language without implying certainty that the
evidence does not support. Route legal, health, financial, security, and current
platform claims to the relevant verified source and reviewer.

## Acceptance

A requirement is ready only when another reviewer can trace it to a need or
decision, understand its boundary and failure case, and write a test or evidence
check without guessing.
