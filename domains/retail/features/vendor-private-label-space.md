# Feature: Vendor Funding, Private Label & Space Productivity

## Description

Supplier economics, private-label development, planogram governance, and space optimisation capabilities that affect margin, availability, product quality, and in-store execution.

## Standard Capabilities

- Vendor master and agreement register with terms, allowance type, rebate basis, co-op funding, scanback, claim evidence, dispute status, and renewal date.
- Trade-spend claim workflow with source sales, purchase, promotion, or compliance evidence.
- Vendor scorecard for service level, fill rate, quality issues, funding recovery, dispute ageing, and margin contribution.
- Private-label product brief, specification, target cost, supplier selection, sample approval, packaging approval, quality hold, and launch gate.
- Landed-cost model for private label including item cost, freight, duty, packaging, inspection, wastage, and allocation basis.
- Planogram repository by store format, fixture, category, shelf, facing, and effective period.
- Space-to-sales and space-to-margin analysis by category, store, fixture, and planogram version.
- Planogram compliance audit with photo evidence, variance reason, corrective action, and rollout status.

## Finance and Control Hooks

- Vendor funding must reconcile to agreements, claims, recoveries, disputes, and accounting treatment.
- Private-label landed cost must be auditable and linked to inventory valuation where finance scope includes cost accounting.
- Planogram and space decisions should expose source metrics and preserve version history for performance review.
