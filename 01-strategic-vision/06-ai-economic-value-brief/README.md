## Objective

Convert AI ambition into a defensible business brief: outcome, workflow, pattern, data, evaluation gates, governance, roadmap. Source brief for PRD, SRS, architecture, test strategy, and AI compliance artefacts.

## Execution Steps

1. Verify `_context/vision.md` exists and an AI ambition is articulated.
2. Invoke `logic.prompt`.
3. Review the brief with the product owner, data owner, and the eventual operating owner — every threshold must be numeric.
