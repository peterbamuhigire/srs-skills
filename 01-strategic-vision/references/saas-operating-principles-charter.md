# SaaS Operating Principles Charter (Phase 01 reference)

A short executive-facing document that captures the operating-model commitments a SaaS company has made. Sourced from Cotton's *How to Run a SaaS Business* essays.

Each principle has: statement, owner, measurement, exception rule.

## 1. Publish pricing

Statement: Tier prices for self-serve plans are published. Enterprise is contact-us only.
Owner: VP Marketing.
Measurement: price page audit quarterly.
Exception: industry-specific custom price holds (regulated).

## 2. Invest in brand

Statement: Brand investment is treated as a measurable line item with target reach, engagement, and authority metrics.
Owner: VP Marketing.
Measurement: monthly brand metrics dashboard.

## 3. Never one sales rep

Statement: A territory or motion that warrants a rep gets at least two reps (so we can compare and survive single attrition).
Owner: VP Sales.
Measurement: org-chart audit.

## 4. Marketing-Sales SLA

Statement: Marketing-Sales hand-off is governed by an SLA with named owners on both sides.
Owner: CRO.
Measurement: SLA dashboard.

## 5. Churn is the quiet killer

Statement: Net retention is a board-level KPI with monthly review and intervention playbooks.
Owner: CCO / CS lead.
Measurement: gross / net retention dashboards.

## 6. Operate on cohort economics

Statement: Decisions about pricing, channel, CAC use cohort math, not aggregate snapshots.
Owner: CFO.
Measurement: cohort dashboards.

## 7. Trust as a product feature

Statement: Trust (security, compliance, reliability) is a product line that has a budget, a roadmap, and a leader.
Owner: CISO.
Measurement: trust-center document pack quarterly.

## 8. Ship one version

Statement: All tenants run the same version of the product. Customisation is via tier-level features, not forks.
Owner: VP Engineering.
Measurement: build inventory.

## 9. Same-day onboarding for self-serve

Statement: A self-serve signup reaches an activation milestone within the first session.
Owner: Product.
Measurement: activation funnel.

## 10. Rule of 40 trajectory

Statement: The business runs a Rule-of-40 model. Boards see the trajectory monthly.
Owner: CFO.
