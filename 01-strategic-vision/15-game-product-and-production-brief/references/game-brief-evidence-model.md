# Game brief evidence model
Back to [the parent skill](../SKILL.md). Use `GHYP-*`, `GEXP-*`, `GRISK-*`, and `GDEC-*`. Each hypothesis records cohort, promise/outcome, source or `VCLAIM-*`, assumption, method, threshold/range, evidence class, owner, review date, contradiction, wellbeing/trust guardrail and pivot/narrow/hold/stop response.

Keep learning, enjoyment, retention, cultural acceptance and commercial viability as separate hypotheses. A cultural record distinguishes fact, fiction, inspiration, consultation, permission, rights and accountable approval. A commercial record exposes price, demand, platform, store/payment/tax, localisation, content throughput, rework, support and downside assumptions.

For ads, state permitted audience/territory, placement, disclosure, explicit choice, cap/pace, decline/close, reward fairness, data/consent by SDK, no-fill/offline/error path and kill switch. A placement is intrusive—and blocked—if it interrupts gameplay, learning, narrative, saving, recovery, pause-to-stop or exit.

The vertical slice tests the highest-risk combination of gameplay, visual pipeline, content pipeline, target hardware, access/safety, culture, throughput and delivery; it is not a miniature content catalogue.
