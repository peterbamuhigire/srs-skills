# PRD Learning Contract

Add this contract to every material product objective or feature group.

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Problem and alternative | State the user/system problem, the non-AI/non-platform alternative, and why the proposed product is smallest useful test |
| Hypothesis | Predict a measurable user, business, technical, safety, or mission outcome |
| Baseline | Name population, period, version, source, denominator and known bias |
| Experiment | Define slice, cohort, owner, duration, reversible change and stop rule |
| Measures | Primary outcome, leading signal, guardrails, counter-metrics and qualitative feedback |
| Decision | Promote, hold, pivot, rollback or stop; name authority and evidence threshold |
| Standardisation | Update requirements, architecture, test, release, support and training only after review |
| Next cycle | Record unresolved uncertainty, follow-up date and owner |

Use a thin slice and short learning cycle. Do not convert a roadmap assumption
into a requirement without evidence. Retain failed tests and rejected hypotheses.
For organisational or facility changes, add charter, decision rights, readiness,
continuity, cutover, stabilisation and lessons-learned fields.
