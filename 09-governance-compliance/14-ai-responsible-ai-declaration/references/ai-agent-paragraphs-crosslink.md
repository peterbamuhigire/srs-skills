# Agent Paragraphs Cross-Link

When the product ships one or more agent features, the public Responsible AI Declaration MUST include an **Agent Section** with the paragraphs prepared by `09-governance-compliance/18-ai-agent-responsible-ai-addendum`.

## Paragraphs to include (plain-language, layperson-reviewable)

1. **What our agents do** — One paragraph per agent feature summarising the does / does-not from the disclosure pack.
2. **Authority** — Our agents act under the workspace's authority and within the limits we publish in the action catalogue.
3. **Audit** — We retain a full audit of every agent action for at least 13 months; for irreversible actions, 7 years.
4. **Human decisions on irreversible actions** — Every action that cannot be cleanly reversed by us requires a human approval at the moment of execution. No exceptions without a documented waiver.
5. **Contestability** — Users can flag any agent action and a human will respond within 2 working days (1 working day for Enterprise tier).
6. **Bias review** — Agents that affect outcomes for protected classes are reviewed on a documented cadence with named reviewers (including external reviewers where regulation requires).

## Evidence trail

Each public paragraph traces to an internal evidence row in `AI_Agent_Responsible_AI_Addendum.md`. Quarterly review verifies every paragraph still maps to an evidence row.

## Public-facing tone

Plain language. No marketing words. No "safe", "trustworthy", or "responsible" without naming the evidence behind the claim. Layperson-reviewed by three non-engineering readers before publication.
