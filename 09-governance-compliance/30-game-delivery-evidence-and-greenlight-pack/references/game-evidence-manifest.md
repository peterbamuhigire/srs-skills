# Game evidence manifest
Back to [the parent skill](../SKILL.md). Record evidence ID/class, path/checksum, build/content/config/SDK versions, producer, reviewer, date, linked `VCLAIM-*`/requirement/architecture/implementation/test/risk/decision, cohort/device/environment/conditions, result, contradiction/failure, limitation, retention, waiver/expiry and disposition.

Use evidence classes exactly: `documented`, `prototype-observed`, `build-executed`, `device-measured`, `player-observed`, `accountable-expert-approved`, and `independently-replicated`. File count, prose, screenshots without identity and self-approval do not raise the class. Preserve rejected claims, failed paths and stop decisions.

The greenlight ledger separately records player value, learning, culture/rights, wellbeing/stopping, access/localisation, security/privacy, device/build, online, ads/economy, commercial assumptions, release/liveops and support. A blocking copyright, factual, safety, privacy, security, access or intrusive-ad finding cannot be waived by forecast revenue.
